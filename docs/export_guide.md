# GROWWW – AI-POWERED INVESTOR DISCOVERY ENGINE
## Export Datasets & Executive Dossiers Specification

---

## 1. Export Catalog & Formats

The Growww Discovery Engine provides reproducible data exports across two primary categories:
1. **Executive PDF Dossiers:** Formatted executive reports generated by pure-Python PDF rendering.
2. **Standardized Research CSV Datasets:** Machine-readable, tabular outputs generated by the discovery and analytics pipelines.

---

## 2. Executive PDF Dossiers

| Document | Target Filename | Size | Key Contents |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weekly Executive Discovery Digest** | `Groww_Weekly_Discovery_Digest.pdf` | ~3.1 KB | Executive KPIs, research corpus health, top 5 recurring SIP friction themes, Groww competitive positioning, and top prioritized opportunities. |
| **Opportunity Prioritization Dossier** | `Groww_Ranked_Opportunities_Dossier.pdf` | ~7.1 KB | Full specifications for all 7 ranked opportunities, problem statements, product interventions, testable hypotheses, verbatim customer quotes, and scoring factors. |

## 3. Standardized CSV Datasets

### 3.1 `ranked_product_opportunities.csv`
- **Location:** `outputs/ranked_product_opportunities.csv`
- **Purpose:** Backlog prioritization ranking.
- **Columns:**
  - `priority_rank`: Integer rank (1 to 7).
  - `opportunity_id`: e.g. `OPP-01`, `OPP-06`.
  - `opportunity_title`: Human-readable initiative name.
  - `opportunity_score`: Composite score out of 10.0.
  - `lifecycle_stage`: Target SIP stage (`S1_SETUP`, `S3_HABIT`, etc.).
  - `target_application`: `Groww` or `Cross-App`.
  - `prevalence_score`: Occurrence weight (0-10).
  - `severity_score`: Customer friction intensity (0-10).
  - `lifecycle_criticality`: Top-of-funnel leverage weight (0-10).
  - `actionability_score`: Engineering & product feasibility (0-10).
  - `evidence_confidence`: Gold-standard validation confidence (0-1).
  - `problem_statement`: Detailed root-cause statement.
  - `proposed_intervention`: Recommended product change.
  - `testable_hypothesis`: Scientific experiment hypothesis.
  - `exemplar_verbatim_quote`: Actual customer review quote.

### 3.2 `theme_frequencies.csv`
- **Location:** `outputs/theme_frequencies.csv`
- **Purpose:** Granular cross-app prevalence benchmarks across 29 canonical themes.
- **Key Columns:** `theme_code`, `theme_name`, `domain`, `total_occurrences`, `prevalence_pct`, `groww_prevalence`, `coin_prevalence`, `paytm_prevalence`, `indmoney_prevalence`.

### 3.3 `lifecycle_journey_analytics.csv`
- **Location:** `outputs/lifecycle_journey_analytics.csv`
- **Purpose:** 8-stage longitudinal SIP drop-off and friction density.
- **Key Columns:** `stage_code`, `stage_name`, `review_count`, `friction_share_pct`, `avg_star_rating`, `criticality_weight`, `drop_off_risk`.

### 3.4 `cross_app_benchmarks.csv`
- **Location:** `outputs/cross_app_benchmarks.csv`
- **Purpose:** 11-dimension competitive comparison across Groww and 7 competitor apps.
- **Key Columns:** `app_name`, `sample_size`, `avg_star_rating`, `one_star_ratio`, `pain_review_ratio`, `rppi_payment_friction`, `rppi_support_failure`, `rppi_mandate_issue`.

### 3.5 `sip_retention_hypotheses.csv`
- **Location:** `outputs/sip_retention_hypotheses.csv`
- **Purpose:** Sensitivity modeling evaluating the 12-month active SIP continuation rate under varying intervention scenarios.
- **Key Columns:** `scenario_name`, `s1_continuation_rate`, `s2_continuation_rate`, `s5_friction_survival`, `projected_12m_active_rate`, `relative_uplift_pct`.

### 3.6 `ai_evidence_log.csv`
- **Location:** `outputs/ai_evidence_log.csv`
- **Purpose:** Immutable audit trail linking all 1,527 AI enrichments with prompt hashes, confidence scores, and verbatim spans.

## 4. Data Governance & Export Safeguards

1. **Zero Unredacted PII:** Reviewer names, email addresses, phone numbers, and bank account details are permanently masked prior to storage and export.
2. **Quota-Free & Unrestricted:** Generated entirely within the local Python runtime without calling external third-party paid APIs.
3. **Role-Enforced:** As detailed in `docs/role_permissions.md`, only `ADMIN`, `RESEARCHER`, and `AUDITOR` roles may export raw customer review datasets.
